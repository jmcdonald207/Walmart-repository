## Reverse DCF result

Walmart's market price implies approximately **22.3% annual FCFF growth for five years**, followed by **2.5% annual terminal growth**.

| Fiscal year | Implied growth | Implied FCFF |
|---|---:|---:|
| Starting FY2026 | — | $17.03 billion |
| Year 1 | 22.3% | $20.82 billion |
| Year 2 | 22.3% | $25.46 billion |
| Year 3 | 22.3% | $31.13 billion |
| Year 4 | 22.3% | $38.07 billion |
| Year 5 | 22.3% | $46.55 billion |
| Year 6 onward | 2.5% | Terminal period |

The 22.3% growth rate is constant during Years 1–5 because the reverse DCF solves for one constant five-year growth assumption. It changes to the 2.5% terminal growth rate after Year 5.
